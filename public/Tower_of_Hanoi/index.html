How to Play Tower of Hanoi

Goal: Move all disks from the left tower to either the middle or right tower.

  • You can only move one disk at a time.
  • You can only pick up the top disk from any tower.
  • A larger disk can never be placed on top of a smaller disk.

Controls: You can either drag and drop the disks or click a tower to select and drop.
