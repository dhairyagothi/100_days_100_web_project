🏥 Basic Remedies

Simple first-aid and self-care guidance while waiting for professional consultation

Emergency Warning

This page is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Seek emergency help immediately for chest pain, breathing difficulty, stroke symptoms, fainting, severe bleeding, severe burns, or rapidly worsening symptoms.

Fever

Rest properly, drink plenty of fluids, and use a cool damp cloth on your forehead if feeling uncomfortable.

⚠️ Seek medical help if the fever is exceptionally high, lasts several days, or comes with confusion, breathing trouble, a stiff neck, or severe dehydration.

Minor Cuts

Wash hands thoroughly, rinse the cut with clean water, apply gentle pressure with a clean cloth if bleeding, then cover with a clean bandage.

⚠️ Get professional medical care if the wound is deep, appears infected, is extremely dirty, or the bleeding does not stop after applying pressure.

Minor Burns

Cool the burn under cool running water for at least 10 minutes, remove tight items (like rings) nearby, and cover loosely with a sterile, non-adhesive dressing.

⚠️ Do not apply ice or butter directly. Seek urgent care for deep, blistered, large, chemical, electrical, or facial/joint burns.

Sprain or Strain

Rest the affected area, apply ice wrapped in a cloth for 15-20 minutes, compress lightly with an elastic bandage, and elevate the limb above the heart.

⚠️ Seek medical help immediately if you experience severe pain, complete numbness, joint deformity, or an absolute inability to walk.

Sore Throat

Drink warm fluids, rest your voice, and gargle with warm salt water periodically to reduce swelling and ease discomfort.

⚠️ Seek medical care if symptoms include high fever, significant breathing or swallowing difficulty, a new rash, or if pain lasts more than a few days.

Dehydration

Sip small amounts of water or an oral rehydration solution slowly and rest in a cool, shaded environment.

⚠️ Seek urgent emergency help for confusion, fainting, severe muscle weakness, extreme lethargy, or persistent vomiting that prevents fluid retention.
