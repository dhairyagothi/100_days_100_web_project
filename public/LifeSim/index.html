Welcome to LifeSim

LifeSim is a unified developer hub designed to house your daily productivity tools, financial dashboards, and exploratory simulations within a cohesive, modular interface.

How to use this platform

  • Home / Welcome: You are here! This section provides an overview of the platform.
  • Focus List: A straightforward to-do list manager. Type your task in the input box and press Enter or click 'Add Task' to keep track of your daily goals.
  • Finance Dashboard: (Currently in Development) A dedicated space to oversee and analyze your financial data and metrics.
  • Life Simulation: A visually engaging implementation of Conway's Game of Life. Watch cellular automata evolve in real-time.

Use the sidebar navigation on the left to seamlessly switch between these modules.
