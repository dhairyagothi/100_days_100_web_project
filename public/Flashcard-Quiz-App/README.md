# Flashcard Quiz App

A simple, interactive flashcard quiz application built with HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript. Perfect for studying and practicing new concepts!

## Features

### 1. Normal Practice Mode
- Browse through your flashcards
- Check answers and get instant feedback
- Add, edit, or delete flashcards
- Save flashcards to localStorage for persistence

### 2. Timed Challenge Mode ⚡
The brand new Timed Challenge Mode adds excitement and urgency to your study sessions!

#### Features of Timed Challenge Mode:
- **Choose Your Duration**: Select between 30, 60, or 120 seconds
- **Countdown Timer**: See the time remaining at the top of the screen
- **Instant Feedback**: Get immediate "Correct!" or "Incorrect!" feedback
- **Auto-Next Card**: Automatically moves to the next card after each answer
- **Live Stats**: Track your progress in real-time:
  - ✅ Correct Answers
  - ❌ Wrong Answers
  - 📊 Total Questions Attempted
  - 🎯 Accuracy Percentage
  - 🏆 Best Timed Score (saved to localStorage)
- **Results Modal**: When time's up, view a comprehensive summary:
  - Final Score
  - Correct and Wrong Answers
  - Accuracy %
  - Total Time Played
  - New Best Score notification (if applicable)
- **Play Again Option**: Restart the challenge and try to beat your best score
- **Locked Editing**: Flashcard editing is disabled during timed mode to prevent distractions

## How to Use

### Normal Practice Mode
1. View the question on the flashcard
2. Type your answer in the input field
3. Click "Check Answer" to see if you got it right
4. Click "Show Answer" to reveal the correct answer
5. Use "Previous" and "Next" buttons to navigate through your flashcards
6. Use the "Add / Edit Flashcard" section to manage your deck

### Timed Challenge Mode
1. Find the "Timed Challenge Mode" section at the top of the page
2. Choose your desired duration from the dropdown (30s, 60s, or 120s)
3. Click "Start Timed Challenge"
4. Answer as many flashcards as you can before time runs out
5. When the challenge ends, view your results in the modal
6. Click "Play Again" to try again, or "Close" to return to normal practice mode

## Technologies Used
