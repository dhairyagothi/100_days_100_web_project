✨ Welcome to Sky Blue FocusRead ✨
This calming blue-themed reader uses AI computer vision (MediaPipe FaceMesh) to track your eyes. When you look away, reading pauses and the last paragraph gets highlighted in soft sky blue.

🧠 How It Works
The AI analyzes 468 points on your face — focusing on your eyes and head position. If you turn away or look down for more than 0.4 seconds, the app automatically highlights where you left off. Look back, and the highlight vanishes!

🎯 Try The Focus Challenge
Read this entire passage without glancing away from the screen. Every time your eyes wander, you'll see a gentle blue highlight appear. It's like having a study buddy who keeps you on track.

🛡️ 100% Private & Local
All face detection runs directly in your browser. No video data is ever sent to any server. Your privacy matters — everything stays on your computer. Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and MediaPipe.

🎮 Controls (Right Panel)
• Clear Highlight — removes the blue highlight manually
• Manual Pause — freeze reading and track manually
The camera shows blue tracking dots — the more dots, the better the detection!

💡 Pro Tips
Sit in a well-lit area and face the camera directly. The sky blue theme reduces eye strain during long reading sessions. Adjust your chair so your face fills most of the camera view.
