SOLAR SYSTEM
SUN
The Sun is the star at the center of our Solar System. It provides the heat and light that make life possible on Earth and contains more than 99% of the Solar System's total mass.
MERCURY
Mercury is the smallest and closest planet to the Sun. It has no atmosphere to retain heat, causing extreme temperature changes between day and night.
VENUS
Venus is the hottest planet in the Solar System due to its thick atmosphere of carbon dioxide. It rotates slowly in the opposite direction of most planets.
EARTH
Earth is the only known planet to support life. About 71% of its surface is covered by water, and it has a protective atmosphere rich in oxygen.
MOON
The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. It influences ocean tides and is the fifth largest moon in the Solar System.
MARS
Mars is known as the Red Planet because of iron oxide on its surface. It has the largest volcano and canyon in the Solar System.
JUPITER
Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System. Its Great Red Spot is a giant storm that has been raging for centuries.
SATURN
Saturn is famous for its spectacular ring system made of ice and rock particles. It is the second largest planet in the Solar System.
URANUS
Uranus rotates on its side, making it unique among planets. It is an icy giant with a pale blue-green color caused by methane in its atmosphere.
NEPTUNE
Neptune is the farthest planet from the Sun. It has the strongest winds in the Solar System, reaching speeds over 2,000 kilometers per hour.
