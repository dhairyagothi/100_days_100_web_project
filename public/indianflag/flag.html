The Meaning Behind the Colours

🧡

Saffron

Represents courage, sacrifice, and the spirit of renunciation. The colour of fire and the rising sun.

⚪

White

Symbolizes peace, truth, and purity. The path of light that guides our nation forward.

🌿

Green

Stands for fertility, growth, and prosperity. Our deep connection with nature and harmony.

⚙️

Ashoka Chakra

The 24-spoke wheel represents eternal motion, righteousness, and the continuous progress of our nation.
