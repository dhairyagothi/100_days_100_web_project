The Mathematics of World Building

Simplex Noise

Instead of pure, chaotic random numbers (white noise), Simplex Noise generates smooth, continuous gradients. This forms the foundational rolling hills and valleys.

Fractal Brownian Motion

By layering multiple "octaves" of noise—each with a higher frequency and lower amplitude—we add realistic jagged details like boulders and rock faces over the smooth hills.

Biome Mapping

The math gives us a grid of numbers between 0 and 1. We map these heights to colors: low values become blue oceans, medium values become green forests, and the highest peaks become white snow.
